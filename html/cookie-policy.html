Cosa sono i cookie

I cookie sono piccoli file di testo che un sito salva sul tuo dispositivo. Strumenti simili, come il local storage del browser, funzionano allo stesso modo e seguono le stesse regole. Si dividono in tecnici, necessari al funzionamento del sito, che non richiedono il consenso, e di profilazione o analisi non anonima, che si possono usare solo dopo il tuo consenso.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. Similar tools, such as the browser's local storage, work the same way and follow the same rules. They fall into technical tools, needed for the site to work, which do not require consent, and profiling or non-anonymous analytics tools, which can only be used after you consent.

Today the site uses technical tools only. It does not use profiling cookies, marketing cookies or statistics that identify you.

Third-party services

Managing your choices

On your first visit a banner lets you accept, reject or choose by category. Closing it with the X continues without accepting. Your choice is remembered for 6 months. You can change it at any time from the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of every page.

You can also delete or block cookies and stored data in your browser settings: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.
